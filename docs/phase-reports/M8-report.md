## 10. Known limitations and what M8b needs from this phase

**Limitations.** Crypto prices stay simulated (labelled SIM). No limit, stop or stop-limit orders, no reservations, no cancel, no partial fills, no order book (all M8b). Crypto accounts still get one snapshot per NYSE trading day (D-62, D-83): no Saturday or Sunday snapshot. The 2% collar, 10 bps and 5 bps are illustrative numbers. `maxQty` for a BUY ignores that the real order uses the fill price at that moment (the collar and the real fill still decide). The preview rate limit is per process.

**What M8b can rely on:**

- **Exact cost per lot.** `position_lots.cost_remaining` (8 dp) is the cost of what is left; the ledger `POSITION_COST` equals its sum, checked with zero tolerance, so partial fills can open one lot per fill and consume lots with the same rule (`consumeLotsFIFO`, `allocateProceeds`) without new rounding rules.
- **One pricing function.** `priceOrder` (engine) is the single place a quantity becomes price, notional and fee; the decision, the preview estimate and `maxQty` all use it. A limit order matcher should reuse it with its limit-aware price.
- **Decision code is the contract.** `decideMarketOrder` and `decideForCommand`/`previewMaxQty` give the same answer for the same state; M8b's new order types need new rules inserted into the documented order, not a second decision path.
- **Fractional quantities end to end:** contract, instrument `qtyStep`/`minQty`, canonical spelling for idempotency hashes, 8-decimal columns (the quantity columns are `numeric(28,10)`).

**What M8b must still do:**

- Orders are inserted once in their final state and never updated (D-49): cancel, partial fills and a working state need that rule revisited (an event-sourced status or a controlled update trigger), with the I5 trigger (filled quantity equals the sum of executions) extended for partial fills.
- Reservations: `RESERVED` ledger accounts, `positions.qty_reserved` (already in the schema), and a reserve formula that uses the 2% collar and the crypto fee (TRADING_ENGINE section 6); `maxQty` for a limit BUY must use the reserved amount.
- Matching against a book needs quantities that can be partly consumed per execution; the zero-cost rule (deviation 4) and the CLAMP (deviation 5) apply to every fill.
- Decide whether crypto should snapshot on calendar days (carried over from M7b).
- Review the illustrative fee and slippage numbers before any non-demo use.
