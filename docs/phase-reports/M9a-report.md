### 5.2 Failures, verbatim, with root cause

**F1. The CSP finding that stopped the phase (before the founder's decision).** Playwright `securitypolicyviolation` events on the terminal with the chart's attribution logo enabled:

```
{"directive":"style-src-elem","blocked":"inline","sample":"","source":".../_next/static/chunks/1ilztjv6wmekg.js"}   (2 events)
{"directive":"style-src-attr","blocked":"inline", ...}   (2 events, document)
```

Root cause: the two `style-src-attr` events were **my own** server-rendered `style={{...}}` attributes (fixed: Tailwind classes). The two `style-src-elem` events come from the library: with `attributionLogo: true` it runs `document.createElement("style")` and sets `innerText` to `a#tv-attr-logo{--fill:#131722;...}`; our `style-src 'self' 'nonce-...'` blocks it and the library has no nonce option. With the logo off: **0 violations**. The founder chose option 2 (logo off; CSP unchanged; no hash allowance). The zero-violation assertion is kept in e2e.

**F2. Integration, second consecutive run in the clone, exit 1:**

```
FAIL |integration| portfolio-consistency.int.test.ts > 4. consistency: reads while orders are placed concurrently (D-63) > 100 portfolio reads while 60 orders are placed in parallel ...
AssertionError: expected 3 to be greater than or equal to 5    (portfolio-consistency.int.test.ts:269)
Tests  1 failed | 540 passed | 4 skipped   Duration 420.69s
```

Root cause (explained, **not reproduced**): the failing line is `expect(distinctCash.size).toBeGreaterThanOrEqual(5)`, a precondition that "the reads really overlapped the orders" and that depends on scheduling speed. The consistency claims of the same test held (`consistent` was 100 of 100 and the identity held for every read), the run was fast (no sleep: 7 minutes; no event-log entry), and nothing in M9a touches the API or the portfolio. With the machine quicker than usual the 60 parallel orders finished while fewer reads were in flight, so only 3 distinct cash values were seen. I could not make it fail again: **10 of 10 isolated runs passed, and two further full integration runs passed**. So acceptance 12 is met by runs 3 and 4 (two consecutive clean runs after the fail), and the earlier failure stays explained-by-reasoning only. This is a test-design weakness (an overlap assertion without enough forced interleaving) that belongs to an older phase; I did not change it.

## 10. Known limitations and what M9b and M10 need

- **Phone (M9b):** below 1024 px the terminal is one stacked column with no horizontal scroll (asserted at 768 and checked at 375 by the older tests), not designed for touch: the watchlist rail becomes a full list below the chart, the ticket is not a bottom sheet, the bottom tabs and the depth book are long. M9b needs: a bottom-sheet ticket (the three optional props `onKindChange`, `pricePick`, `sideCommand` and `onSettled` are the interface), a compact watchlist, and a phone chart height. `TerminalChrome` owns the header; `InstrumentView` owns the grid.
- **Chart:** polling only (the forming candle refreshes every 5 s, not on every quote); no drawing tools or indicators; the axis labels are rounded by design (the legend is exact); the in-chart logo is off (D-103); a stale quote unmounts the chart and a fresh one creates it again (once per episode).
- **Orders list poll** is fixed at 3 s and duplicated by the terminal's counts (one instance only: the single `OrdersList` serves both tabs).
- **Watchlist** is per browser and unsynced; a symbol removed on another device reappears there.
- **Integration test F2** (an overlap precondition) should be given forced interleaving before it flakes again.
- **Typed-before-ready:** not reproducible at 4× and 6×; keep `typed-before-ready.spec.ts` in the suite and re-check if the form is rebuilt for the phone sheet.
- **For M10 (demo polish):** the screenshots in `docs/phase-reports/m9a/` are at 1440, 1024 and 768 and regenerate with `UPDATE_SCREENSHOTS=1`; the notices page exists; the page title carries the price; real Lighthouse numbers on a deployed build were not measured.
