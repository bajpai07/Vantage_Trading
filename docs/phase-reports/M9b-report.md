### 6.2 Other pages on a phone (T4): what I looked at and what I changed

The layout test visits login, register, third-party notices, portfolio, orders, activity and the not-found page at the five viewports and checks horizontal scroll, the amber bar, the fixed bars against the footer, touch targets and input sizes. Findings:

- **Header search box** (every private page): its font was 13 px, which makes iOS zoom the page on focus. Now 16 px on a phone.
- **All text inputs** (`inputClass`): 14 px; now 16 px under 768 px (login, register, ticket quantity and limit price, search).
- **Footer**: its bottom padding (4 rem) was enough for the tab bar but not for the safe-area inset; it is now `4rem + env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`; the tab bar has the inset as padding.
- **Viewport meta**: there was none beyond Next's default; it is now `width=device-width, initial-scale=1, viewport-fit=cover` (no `user-scalable`).
- **Terminal pages**: the header was sticky under the banner, which is wrong when the banner wraps to three lines; on a phone it is no longer sticky.
- **Found fine, unchanged:** no horizontal scroll or clipped primary control on login, register, notices, portfolio, orders, activity or the error/not-found pages; login and register already had the right `autocomplete` values (`email`, `current-password`, `new-password`, `nickname`, `country`); tables (positions, open orders, history, portfolio) already scroll inside their own container.

## 9. Deviations from the mock and open questions

1. **The ticket gained four optional props**, beyond the four the prompt named: `kindCommand` (book tap opens Limit mode), `onPhaseChange` (read-only status for the Close lock), and `keepAttemptOnDismiss` with `reopenSignal`. The last two change what Esc does on the review dialog **on a phone only**, after an uncertain outcome: it hides the dialog and keeps the attempt and key (before, it discarded them). Without that, D-108's "dismissing after an uncertain outcome keeps the key" is unreachable, because the native modal review dialog covers the sheet's Close button. An explicit Cancel button still discards, as in M6b. The idempotency lifecycle, review/confirm flow and error mapping are otherwise untouched. Please confirm.
2. **Landscape phones (844 px) get the tablet layout**, because the rule is "phone < 768 px" (D-107); the 844x390 viewport has no horizontal scroll and was screenshotted (`landscape-844x390.png`) but it is not the sheet layout.
3. **No chip strip** of symbols above the chart (it crowds the header at 360 px); the watchlist is a button and a full-screen drawer.
4. **Mock details:** the mock's "Review order" title and "Market · whole shares" line are not used (the real ticket is shown); the bottom tab bar replaces the mock's lack of one; the mock's sparkline is not used (the real chart is).
5. **Phone book levels:** the phone asks the API for `levels=8`, then 15, then 25 (the depth route's cost is 1 up to 15 levels and 2 above), so the server's `onBook` and `level` match what is shown.
6. Commits are not one per task (section 3). The T0a commit is separate.
7. Screenshots in `docs/phase-reports/m9b/`: `instrument-390.png`, `sheet-390.png`, `drawer-390.png`, `orders-390.png`, `portfolio-390.png`, `landscape-844x390.png` (written only with `UPDATE_SCREENSHOTS=1`; a normal run leaves the tree clean, shown by the clone's empty `git status`).

## 10. Known limitations and what M10 needs

- **Limits:** the sheet is not a swipe-to-dismiss surface (optional in the prompt, not built); the orders lists still poll rather than push; the segmented control's History tab loads its first answer when the panel mounts; positions and orders are tables that scroll inside their container, not stacked cards; the bottom bars take about 112 px of a 640 px screen.
- **For M10 (demo polish, deployment, README, video):** the phone project of the e2e suite and `UPDATE_SCREENSHOTS=1` produce phone screenshots and flows for the demo video; `ORDERS_POLL_MS`, `QUOTE_POLL_MS` and `PORTFOLIO_POLL_MS` are the three web polling settings to set on a deployed build; the notices page and footer link must stay reachable; `TRUST_FORWARDED_FOR` and `TRUSTED_PROXY_CIDRS` must be set correctly behind the real proxy because the public depth route is limited per address.
- **Manual checklist for a real phone against a deployed build:**
  1. Open the instrument page on iOS Safari and Android Chrome; the amber bar is visible at the top and the Buy / Sell bar sits above the tab bar without touching the home indicator.
  2. Tap Buy: the sheet rises, focus is in it, the page behind does not scroll. Tap the quantity field: the keyboard opens, the sheet moves above it, and the Review button is still reachable by scrolling inside the sheet. No zoom on focus.
  3. Review and Confirm a small market order; the result shows exact strings; close the result and the sheet closes.
  4. Turn the phone to landscape with the sheet open and with an order being sent (the order and its key must survive).
  5. Switch to airplane mode between Confirm and the answer; check "Retry safely", dismiss with the back swipe or Esc, reopen the sheet, retry, and confirm a single order in Orders.
  6. Swipe vertically starting on the chart: the page scrolls. Drag horizontally on the chart: it pans. Pinch: it zooms. Tap a candle: the legend shows its strings.
  7. Open the Watchlist drawer, star and unstar, choose a symbol (the drawer closes).
  8. Background the tab for a minute and return: the quotes, orders and portfolio refresh at once.
  9. Check the footer's "Third-party notices" link is reachable at the bottom of every page.
